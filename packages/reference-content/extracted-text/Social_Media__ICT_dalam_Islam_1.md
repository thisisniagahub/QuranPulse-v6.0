 The advent of social media has connected East and West, 
North and South, in an open exchange of information and 
communication via Facebook, WhatsApp, Twitter, and a slew 
of other platforms.

fragments them simultaneously.
 Artificial Intelligence (AI) begins as automation of processes, 
readily seen in industry and retail, but can grow to being 
integral part of manufacturing processes, banking, advance 
medicine, and war.

materials and raw events of higher order, and unconnected facts
