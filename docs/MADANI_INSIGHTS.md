## 1. Executive Summary
The 2024 Madani Convention highlights the critical need for "Values in the Digital Era" to combat the challenges of **Post Normal Times (PNT)**—characterized by Chaos, Complexity, and Contradictions.

QuranPulse v6.0 will adopt the **Madani Framework** to evolve from a content app to a **Civilizational Ecosystem (Membangun Tamadun)**. Our goal is to cultivate the "Insan Madani" (Civilized Human).

## 2. Core Strategic Pillars

### A. The "3R" User Reputation System
*Derived from the definition of a "Muslim yang Disegani".*

We will gamify positive community contribution through the **3R Badge System**:
1.  **Relevan (Relevant):**
    *   *Metric:* Consistent contribution of high-quality content (e.g., sharing verified hadith, reflections).
    *   *Goal:* Users stay relevant across time, contributing to the "Extended Present".
2.  **Dihormati (Respected):**
    *   *Metric:* High "Adab Score". Low toxicity, high helpfulness ratings.
    *   *Goal:* Ensuring safety from "gangguan lidah dan tangan" (cyberbullying/toxicity).
3.  **Dirujuk (Referred):**
    *   *Metric:* Content is frequently saved/shared by others. User is recognized as a subject matter expert (SME).
    *   *Goal:* Becoming a pillar of the community.

### B. The Al-Hujurat Digital Protocol (17 Values)
*The app's Community Guidelines and AI Moderation will be strictly based on the 17 values extracted from Surah Al-Hujurat:*

*   **Tabayyun (Verify):** Auto-flagging unverified viral content.
*   **No Mockery/Name-calling:** AI toxicity detection.
*   **Fa Ashlihu (Reconcile):** Tools for de-escalating comment section conflicts.
*   **Taqwa & Justice:** The foundation of all interactions.

### C. The "Insan Sejahtera" Tracker (Holistic Well-being)
*Moving beyond spiritual tracking to 8-Dimensional Wellness:*

1.  **Spiritual (Rohani):** Quran/Prayer tracking (Core).
2.  **Intellectual:** Learning streaks (Iqra module).
3.  **Physical:** Health integration (future).
4.  **Social:** Community engagement.
5.  **Financial:** Zakat/Sadaqah tracking.
6.  **Career:** Professional development (Ethics).
7.  **Environmental:** Eco-conscious habits.
8.  **Emotional:** Mental health checks.

## 3. Advanced Tools & "Next Level" Features

### 🧭 Tool 1: The "Madani Compass" (AI Advisor)
*Addressing the "BANI" world (Brittle, Anxious, Nonlinear, Incomprehensible).*

*   **Concept:** An AI companion that acts as "Heartware" (Perkakasan Hati).
*   **Functionality:**
    *   *User Input:* "I feel anxious about the future."
    *   *AI Response:* Prescribes specific "Digital Prescriptions" (e.g., Surah Ad-Duha for hope, Zikir for anxiety) based on the "Madani Compass" moral bearings.

### 🛡️ Tool 2: The "Tabayyun Engine"
*Combatting "Disinformation, Misinformation, Mal-information".*

*   **Concept:** A crowdsourced truth-verification system.
*   **Mechanism:**
    *   Viral Islamic quotes/videos are submitted.
    *   "Digital Huffaz" (Verified Scholars/Students) review and vote.
    *   Content is stamped: **Sahih** (Authentic), **Palsu** (Fake), or **Tiada Asas** (Baseless).

### 🔮 Tool 3: "Predictive Fiqh" (Beta)
*Addressing the "Speed" of the Digital Era.*

*   **Concept:** Using AI to anticipate emerging ethical dilemmas before they become widespread crises.
*   **Functionality:**
    *   Scans trending topics (e.g., "AI Art Copyright", "Crypto Staking").
    *   Matches them with *Qawaid Fiqhiyyah* (General Islamic Legal Maxims).
    *   Provides "Preliminary Guidance" (not Fatwa) to help users navigate uncertainty.
