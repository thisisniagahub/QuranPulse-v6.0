## 1. The Philosophy: From Data to Wisdom (Hikmah)

We are upgrading QuranPulse from a simple "Knowledge Provider" to a "Wisdom Enabler".

*   **Data:** 6236 Ayats (Raw).
*   **Information:** Translation & Tafsir (Processed).
*   **Knowledge:** Fiqh & Hukum (Contextualized).
*   **Wisdom (Hikmah):** Application of knowledge to purify the soul (Tazkiyah) and build a harmonious society (Madani).

---

## 2. Advanced Tools: The "Surah Al-Hujurat" Suite

We propose 3 new AI Engines designed to enforce Islamic Ethics (Adab) automatically.

### 🛠️ Tool A: The "Tabayyun Engine" (Fact Verifier)
*   **Guideline 6:** *"O you who believe! If a Fasiq comes to you with any news, verify it..."* (49:6).
*   **Problem:** Fake Hadith & Viral Hoaxes on WhatsApp.
*   **Solution:** An AI Agent that cross-references user queries against `semak.hadith.gov.my` and `muftiwp.gov.my`.
*   **Workflow:**
    1.  User forwards text to Tok Imam Bot.
    2.  AI detects "News/Claim".
    3.  **Action:** Search Official Sources -> Verify Sanad/Status.
    4.  **Output:** "Status: PALSU (Maudu'). Jangan sebarkan."

### 🛠️ Tool B: "Adab Sentinel" (Ethical Moderator)
*   **Guideline 11:** *"Let not a people ridicule another people..."* (49:11).
*   **Guideline 12:** *"Avoid much suspicion..."* (49:12).
*   **Problem:** Toxic comments in "Moments" or "Groups".
*   **Solution:** Real-time NLP filter trained on Islamic Adab.
*   **Mechanism:**
    *   Detects **Sarkasme** (Sarcasm), **Ghibah** (Backbiting), and **Namimah** (Slander).
    *   **Action:** Instead of just blocking, AI gives *Nasihat*: "Sahabat, komen ini berbaur sangkaan buruk. Allah melarang kita..."

### 🛠️ Tool C: "The Islah Protocol" (Conflict Resolver)
*   **Guideline 9:** *"Make peace between them..."* (49:9).
*   **Application:** Automated dispute resolution for "Souq" (Marketplace) transactions.
*   **Workflow:**
    *   Buyer complains about Seller.
    *   AI acts as "Mediator" (Orang Tengah).
    *   Suggests fair solutions based on *Fiqh Muamalat* (e.g., Khiyar Aib - Option to return defect goods).
