# Meaningful Metrics: Tracking Iqra Progress

How we measure success and keep users addicted (in a good way).

## 1. User Metrics (Visible)

### 📈 Mastery Level (Syahadah)
* **Iqra 1:** 0-100%
* **Definition:** % of pages completed with 3-stars.

### 🔥 Streak (Istiqamah)
* **Definition:** Consecutive days with at least 5 minutes of practice.
* **Reward:** Unlock special themes or avatars (e.g., "Neon Quran").

### 🏆 Fluency Score (Fasohah)
* **Calculation:** `(Correct Attempts / Total Attempts) * 100`
* **Feedback:** "Makhraj anda 90% tepat hari ini!"

## 2. System Metrics (Invisible/Admin)

### 📉 Drop-off Rate
* Where do users quit? (e.g., Page 15 is too hard).
* **Action:** If 50% fail Page 15, we assume the UI/Teaching is bad and fix it.

### ⏱️ Time-to-Mastery
* Average time to finish Iqra 1.
* **Goal:** Reduce this over time with better AI prompts.
